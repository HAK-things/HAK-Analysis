Instructions

Both programs run directly in your browser. Choose Individual Analysis for a single player or Team Analysis for the entire team.

Individual Analysis

  1. Enter the player name, date and opponent.
  2. Add the match video and create the metrics you need.
  3. For every action, choose a metric and a rating: TOP, VG, G, R, M, BM or MFG.
  4. Use the Action Log to review, filter and edit situations and coaching notes.
  5. Select the situations you want and generate their video clips.
  6. Use Play All Selected Clips to review them in sequence.
  7. Export PDF creates a complete report, while CSV saves the table containing all actions.
  8. Export GKA Package creates a portable analysis with the selected generated clips. The recipient can load it using Import.
Important: Routine appears in the report but does not affect the final rating.

Team Analysis

  1. Enter the team or match name, opponent and date, then add the video.
  2. Create metrics for the tactical situations you want to track.
  3. Select one or more metrics and press START CLIP at the beginning of the situation.
  4. Press END CLIP at the end, then add a coaching note or skip it.
  5. The Positive/Negative rating can be assigned immediately or later through Edit.
  6. Filter the situations, select the ones you need and arrange them with the arrow controls.
  7. Generate, review and download individual or combined clips.
  8. Export TMA creates a package with the selected clips. Add TMA combines several projects into one selection.
  9. CSV contains all situations, while PDF creates a summary team report.
Tip: Before exporting GKA or TMA, make sure every selected situation has a generated clip.

Индивидуален анализ

  1. Въведете името на играча, датата и съперника.
  2. Добавете видеото от мача и създайте нужните метрики.
  3. За всяко действие изберете метрика и оценка: TOP, VG, G, R, M, BM или MFG.
  4. Използвайте Action Log, за да преглеждате, филтрирате и редактирате ситуациите и треньорските бележки.
  5. Изберете ситуациите, които искате, и генерирайте видео клиповете им.
  6. Използвайте Play All Selected Clips, за да ги прегледате последователно.
  7. Export PDF създава пълен отчет, а CSV запазва таблицата с всички действия.
  8. Export GKA Package създава преносим анализ с избраните генерирани клипове. Получателят може да го зареди чрез Import.
Важно: Routine се показва в отчета, но не влияе върху крайната оценка.

Отборен анализ

  1. Въведете името на отбора или мача, съперника и датата, след което добавете видеото.
  2. Създайте метрики за тактическите ситуации, които искате да следите.
  3. Изберете една или повече метрики и натиснете START CLIP в началото на ситуацията.
  4. Натиснете END CLIP в края, след което добавете треньорска бележка или я пропуснете.
  5. Оценката Positive/Negative може да бъде зададена веднага или по-късно чрез Edit.
  6. Филтрирайте ситуациите, изберете нужните и ги подредете със стрелките.
  7. Генерирайте, преглеждайте и сваляйте отделни или обединени клипове.
  8. Export TMA създава пакет с избраните клипове. Add TMA обединява няколко проекта в една селекция.
  9. CSV съдържа всички ситуации, а PDF създава обобщен отборен отчет.
Съвет: Преди Export GKA или TMA се уверете, че всяка избрана ситуация има генериран клип.

Goalkeeping Coach · UEFA Goalkeeper A

My name is Nikolay Kutsarov. I am a goalkeeping coach with extensive experience in youth, senior and professional football, and I hold a UEFA Goalkeeper A licence.

My coaching career began at DFK Tigrite, where I worked with the youngest age groups and laid the foundations of my approach to teaching and developing young players.

I continued my professional development at Botev Plovdiv as goalkeeping coach of the second team, which competed in Bulgaria's Second Professional League.

At CSKA 1948, I progressed through every major level of the club structure—from the elite youth groups and the teams in the Third and Second Leagues to the first team. This journey gave me valuable experience both in developing young goalkeepers and in meeting the demands of professional football at the highest level.

I am currently a goalkeeping coach at the PFC Levski Sofia Academy, working with elite youth teams.

My work focuses on developing the modern goalkeeper: defending the goal and the space, one-on-one situations, crosses, the goalkeeper's offensive play and effective decision-making in real match situations.

For me, video analysis is an essential part of the training process. It allows goalkeeper and team performance to be evaluated objectively, in detail and with clarity. This principle is the foundation of the HAK Analysis platform.
